Architecture layout language:

- Left-to-right pipeline.
- 3-6 large stage cards as primary nodes.
- Substeps as small tags inside or below each card.
- Thin connectors with directional arrows.
- A single visible terminal artifact on the right.
- Optional cross-cutting tracks drawn as separate horizontal lanes underneath.
- Generous spacing; cards must not touch.
- No numbered stage badges unless the brief asks for them.
